The real leak

Cars and trust get lost in the gaps.

Not because people don’t care — because the handoffs are thin. Marketing to call to lead to appointment to VCC to estimate to paid RO to workflow to pickup to review. Every gap is a place a car (or a relationship) slips away.

Revenue leaks at handoffs

Missed callbacks, soft appointments, estimates that never close, sold work that doesn’t get scheduled cleanly.

Trust erodes in silence

Customer didn’t hear back. Status unclear. Surprise at pickup. That’s how good shops still get average reviews.

Leadership flies blind

You find out late. Spreadsheets and gut feel instead of a live pulse on conversion, cash, and capacity.
