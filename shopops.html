Leadership pulse

No metrics without an action.

A number that doesn’t change what someone does next is just a report. Every pulse in ShopOps answers three questions — then makes the action easy to take.

So what? Is that good or bad? What now?

Finger on the pulse

Today’s flow — calls, shows, approvals, cars in process. Where the chain is healthy, and where the next action is late.

Know your numbers

Refresh invoices from AMS and you already know the day and the week — no waiting on the WBR sometime Monday. Revenue, profit, margin: good or bad against the week you needed, and what to do about it.

Know the math

GPPH without the spreadsheet. On the AMS repair order, in front of you. If the margin is bad, what now is a pricing or dispatch decision — not a spreadsheet after the car is gone.

What if

Staffing, pricing, capacity — try it before you change the shop. A scenario only counts if it tells you what to do next.
