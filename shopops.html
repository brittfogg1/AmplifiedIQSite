The problem

Shop operations run on disconnected systems and tribal knowledge

Most auto repair shops piece together work orders, estimates, invoicing, scheduling, and financial reporting across multiple systems—or track it on paper and whiteboards. Parts margins get lost. Profitability per repair order is a guess. Critical operational knowledge lives in a few people's heads.

Repair orders and estimates scattered across paper and software
No real-time visibility into job profitability or shop P&L
Inventory, vendor pricing, and parts margins tracked manually
Customer history and vehicle records disconnected from workflow
Time clock, scheduling, and technician coordination on whiteboards

What ShopOps does

Complete shop operations in one system

ShopOps unifies repair orders, estimates, scheduling, invoicing, inventory, and financial reporting into one platform built for how auto repair shops actually operate. From customer intake to invoicing and P&L analysis—all in one place.

Repair Orders

ROs, Estimates & Job Groups

Create repair orders, build estimates with line items and job groups, track labor and parts through completion.

Customers & Vehicles

Customer & Vehicle Records

Manage customer information, vehicle history, service records, and repair history in one unified database.

Scheduling

Appointments & Time Clock

Schedule customer appointments, track technician availability, manage time clock for accurate labor tracking.

Technicians

Team & Time Management

Technician profiles, time clock integration, job assignments, and labor tracking for accurate costing.

Inventory

Vendors, Parts & Margins

Vendor management, parts price lists with margin tracking, inventory oversight—know your parts profitability.

Financials

Invoicing, P&L & RO Profit

Generate invoices, track shop P&L, see profitability per repair order with real-time financial dashboards.

Customer Portal

Self-Service Customer Access

Customer-facing portal for appointment scheduling, service history, estimates, and communication.

AI-assisted capabilities: Call recording, transcription, summarization, and call coaching features available to help capture customer interactions and improve service advisor performance.

How it's built

Built in a working AAMCO shop, for shop operators

ShopOps is developed by the owner of AAMCO of Springdale / Shiloh Automotive using the Amplified IQ process—Ignite → Forge → Enable → Amplify. It's not generic business software adapted for auto repair. It's operations software built from the shop floor up.

🔥 Ignite: Discovered in the bay

Built from real shop pain points identified by a working franchise owner. Requirements come from daily operations—repair orders, estimates, invoicing, parts margins, technician scheduling—not assumptions about how shops should work.

🔨 Forge: Modern web platform

Cloud-based platform accessible from the shop floor, service desk, or office. Multi-user, role-based access. Theme configuration supports AAMCO white-label branding (carmine/red) alongside the default dark UI—proof it can adapt to franchise branding without claiming official endorsement.

🛡️ Enable: Shop-tested daily

Refined in a live AAMCO franchise environment. Workflows tested against real customers, real repair orders, real invoicing, and real technician feedback. Features ship when they work on the shop floor.

📣 Amplify: Built to scale

Architecture designed for multi-shop operations. Built for deployment to other AAMCO franchise owners and independent shop operators who need operations intelligence, not operational overhead.
