What AgModel does

Production intelligence for breeder operations

AgModel brings structure, visibility, and forecasting to breeder flock management. Track flocks from rearing through laying, compare performance to targets, and generate settlements with confidence.

Hierarchy

Company → Complex → Farm → House

Model your full operation structure. Track performance at every level, roll up to executive dashboards.

Genetics

Breeds & Genetic Lines

Manage genetic line data, track breed performance, maintain accurate production profiles by line.

Lifecycle

Rearing → Laying

Full flock lifecycle tracking from placement through closeout. Movement history, performance curves, and event timelines.

Forecasting

Targets & Projections

Set production targets by house and line. Compare actuals to forecast. See where you're ahead or behind.

Dashboards

Flock, Farm, Complex, Executive

Purpose-built dashboards for each operational level. Real-time visibility into what matters at that altitude.

Reports

Data export

CSV Export for Analysis

Export production data, forecasts, and settlements to CSV for deeper analysis or integration with other systems.
