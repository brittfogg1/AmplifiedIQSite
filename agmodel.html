AgModel: Poultry management from hatch to placement

A web-based platform helping poultry breeders manage raising chickens from hatch through placement at laying or broiler farms.

Built with AI + human intelligence. Designed for subscription deployment from day one.

Request a demo See what it does

AgModel is the first product built on the Amplified IQ platform. It evolved from a legacy MS Access application into a modern, web-based system ready for multi-customer deployment.

What AgModel does

Manage the full hatch-to-placement lifecycle

AgModel tracks and coordinates every step in raising poultry from day-old chicks through placement at production farms. Built to handle the complexity of modern breeding operations.

Hatch management

Track hatch data and outcomes

Record hatch dates, quantities, genetics, and quality metrics. Know exactly what came from each hatch and where it went.

Flock tracking

Monitor growth and health

Track weight gain, feed consumption, mortality, and health events across multiple flocks and facilities.

Placement coordination

Schedule and execute placement

Coordinate bird readiness, customer orders, transport logistics, and farm capacity for smooth placement operations.

Who it's for

Built for poultry breeding operations

AgModel is designed for poultry breeders and integrators managing hatch-to-placement operations—whether raising birds for laying hen production or broiler placement.

For breeding operations

If you're managing day-old chicks through grow-out and placement, AgModel gives you centralized visibility and coordination across your operation.

  • Track multiple hatches and flocks simultaneously
  • Coordinate placement schedules with customer demand
  • Monitor growth performance and feed efficiency
  • Manage health events and interventions
  • Generate reports for internal and customer visibility

For integrators and partners

AgModel helps bridge breeding operations with downstream customers—laying farms, broiler farms, and integrated production systems.

  • Visibility into bird availability and readiness
  • Clear placement schedules and logistics coordination
  • Quality and performance data for decision-making
  • Shared accountability across the supply chain
  • Subscription-ready for multi-site deployment
