Built on the Amplified IQ platform—AmpIQ Sales Planning helps retail operations teams generate accurate item-level forecasts without the complexity or learning curve of traditional demand planning systems.

The problem

Item-level forecasting is too slow, too complex, or too opaque

Retail operations teams need item-level sales forecasts to plan inventory, shipments, and promotions. But traditional demand planning tools are expensive, require weeks of training, and hide their forecasting methods. Spreadsheets are manual and error-prone. Teams need fast, transparent forecasts they can trust and adjust.

Traditional demand planning tools require weeks of training and setup
Forecasting methods are opaque—you don't know why a number was chosen
Manual overrides get lost; no accountability for statistical vs human judgment
New item launches are guesswork—no way to map to similar existing items
Scenario planning wrecks the base forecast; no clean way to model what-ifs

What AmpIQ Sales Planning does

Fast, transparent item-level forecasting for retail operations

AmpIQ Sales Planning generates item-level sales forecasts at the item, channel, and week level with minimal setup. Automatic best-fit statistical methods, manual overrides tied to business events, scenario planning that doesn't wreck your base forecast—and full transparency into why each forecast was chosen.

Forecast Mode

Statistical Forecasting with Method Transparency

Automatic best-fit method selection per item. VDP-based forecasting (Velocity × Distribution × Price). See why each method was chosen, confidence scores, and accuracy metrics.

Manual Overrides

Human Judgment with Accountability

Override statistical forecasts and tie changes to business events (promotions, launches, retirements). Track statistical vs human override performance over time.

New Items

New-Item Mapping to Existing Items

Map new item launches to similar existing items. Forecast new items based on historical performance of comparable products—no more guesswork.

Planning Mode

Scenario Planning Without Wrecking Base Forecast

Model new item launches, retirements, distribution changes, velocity shifts, and promotions. Include/exclude scenarios; merge into base forecast when ready (admin-controlled).

Analytics

Sales & Forecast Timeline with Accuracy Scoring

See actuals, statistical forecasts, and scenario impact over time. Accuracy scoring flags items needing review. Compare statistical vs override performance.

Shipment Forecasting

Shipment Forecasts from Sales Forecasts

Generate shipment forecasts from item-level sales forecasts using item lead times. Plan inventory and logistics from the same forecast model.

Data Privacy

Local Storage—No Cloud Upload

Sales data stays on your device. Client-side web app with local file import. No cloud upload for core forecasting—your data doesn't leave your control.
